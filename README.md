# 🎵 Mega Music Maker 🎵

A full music production studio so simple a 5-year-old can use it — and powerful enough to actually make songs.

**Just open `index.html` in any browser. That's it.** No installs, no downloads, no accounts. Every sound is synthesized live in the browser with the Web Audio API.

## What's inside

| Studio section | What it does |
|---|---|
| 🥁 **Sound Pads** | 12 big colorful pads — drums, zaps, boings, robots. Tap to play (keys `1-8` / `q w e r` work too). |
| 🤖 **Beat Machine** | A 16-step, 6-track drum sequencer. Paint squares to build a beat, drag the 🐢→🐇 slider for speed, or press **✨ Magic Beat** to instantly generate a groove that always sounds good. |
| 🌈 **Rainbow Piano** | 8 pentatonic keys (`a s d f g h j k`) — pentatonic means there are **no wrong notes**, everything sounds nice together. |
| 🎤 **My Sounds** | A real mic sampler: hold a pad, make a silly sound, let go — then tap to play it back. |
| 💿 **Song Recorder** | One red button records everything you play into a real audio file you can replay and save. |

## Why it's kid-proof

- Giant touch-friendly buttons with emoji — no reading required
- Sparkles ✨ fly out of everything you tap
- Pentatonic scale + Magic Beat = impossible to sound bad
- Works on phones, tablets, and computers
